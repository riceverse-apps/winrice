# Fastfetch

## 1. What it is

Fastfetch prints a nice summary of your computer next to a logo, a classic piece of any "rice" screenshot.

## 2. Who it's for

Anyone who likes a polished terminal.

## 3. What changes on your screen

A summary of your system (OS, CPU, memory) is printed each time PowerShell starts.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Enable it and open a new PowerShell window.

## 5. Main shortcuts

No shortcuts of its own.

## 6. Common customizations

Run `fastfetch --help` to see how to change the logo and the info shown.

## 7. Common problems

Nothing shows: open a new terminal window.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle; the added line is removed.
