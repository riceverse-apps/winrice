# Lively Wallpaper

## 1. What it is

Lively Wallpaper plays videos, GIFs and interactive web pages as your desktop background.

## 2. Who it's for

Anyone who wants a living desktop.

## 3. What changes on your screen

Your wallpaper becomes a video, animation or web page.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Enable it and pick a wallpaper from its library.

## 5. Main shortcuts

No shortcuts of its own.

## 6. Common customizations

Set pause rules, audio and performance in its settings.

## 7. Common problems

High CPU use: enable pause when an app is in focus.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle; your normal wallpaper returns.
