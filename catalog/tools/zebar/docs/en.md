# Zebar

## 1. What it is

Zebar draws a status bar (clock, workspaces, CPU, battery) at the edge of your screen. It is built with web technologies, so it is very customizable.

## 2. Who it's for

Anyone who wants a nicer top or bottom bar, especially together with GlazeWM.

## 3. What changes on your screen

A new bar appears on screen. It may sit above the Windows taskbar.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Enable it and check the bar. Widget packs can be chosen from the Zebar settings.

## 5. Main shortcuts

None.

## 6. Common customizations

Pick another widget pack or edit its HTML/CSS to change colors and items.

## 7. Common problems

The bar overlaps the taskbar: hide the Windows taskbar with a Windhawk mod.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle and the bar closes.
