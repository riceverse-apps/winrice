# Rainmeter

## 1. What it is

Rainmeter shows customizable widgets called skins on your desktop, such as clocks, CPU graphs and music players.

## 2. Who it's for

People who want a decorated, informative desktop.

## 3. What changes on your screen

Widgets appear on the desktop wallpaper area.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Enable it, right-click its tray icon and load skins from the list.

## 5. Main shortcuts

No shortcuts of its own.

## 6. Common customizations

Download skins (`.rmskin`) and edit their `.ini` files.

## 7. Common problems

A skin shows nothing: refresh it from the tray menu.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle; the widgets disappear.
