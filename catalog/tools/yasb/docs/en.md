# YASB Reborn

## 1. What it is

YASB is a customizable status bar for Windows, styled with CSS and configured in a YAML file.

## 2. Who it's for

People who want a macOS/Linux-style top bar.

## 3. What changes on your screen

A bar appears at the top of the screen with clock, workspaces and system widgets.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Install a Nerd Font, enable YASB and edit `config.yaml` in `.config\yasb`.

## 5. Main shortcuts

No shortcuts of its own.

## 6. Common customizations

Widgets in `config.yaml`; colors and fonts in `styles.css`.

## 7. Common problems

Icons look like boxes: install a Nerd Font. Bar hidden behind the taskbar: hide the taskbar with a Windhawk mod.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle; the bar closes.
