# Seelen UI

## 1. What it is

Seelen UI is an all-in-one desktop environment for Windows with a bar, a dock, an app launcher and tiling.

## 2. Who it's for

People who want a macOS-like desktop without assembling many tools.

## 3. What changes on your screen

A top bar and a dock appear, and windows can be tiled.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Enable it and open its settings from the tray icon to choose layouts and themes.

## 5. Main shortcuts

No shortcuts of its own.

## 6. Common customizations

Bar items, dock apps, tiling layouts and themes.

## 7. Common problems

Do not run it with another bar or tiling manager at the same time.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle.
