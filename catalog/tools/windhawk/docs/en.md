# Windhawk

## 1. What it is

Windhawk is a store of small *mods* that change how Windows looks and behaves, such as a restyled taskbar. WinRice installs the platform; you choose the mods.

## 2. Who it's for

Anyone who wants to restyle the taskbar, Start menu or notification center without technical skills.

## 3. What changes on your screen

Nothing at first. After enabling a mod (for example Windows 11 Taskbar Styler) the taskbar or menu changes.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Open Windhawk from the tray icon, search for "Windows 11 Taskbar Styler" and click Install.

## 5. Main shortcuts

None.

## 6. Common customizations

Taskbar Styler has ready-made styles; Taskbar Labels shows window titles; there are mods to hide the taskbar.

## 7. Common problems

Explorer crashes after a mod: disable that mod in Windhawk. After a Windows update a mod may stop working until it is updated.

## 8. How to undo

Disable the mod in Windhawk, or turn off the toggle. Uninstalling Windhawk removes all mods.
