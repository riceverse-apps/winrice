# Komorebi

## 1. What it is

Komorebi is a *tiling window manager*: it places your windows side by side automatically. It is lighter than GlazeWM but is configured by files and a command line.

## 2. Who it's for

Advanced users who want a very light tiling setup.

## 3. What changes on your screen

Windows are arranged automatically in a grid.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Enable it, then open a few apps. Edit `komorebi.json` in your user folder to change the layout.

## 5. Main shortcuts

No shortcuts of its own.

## 6. Common customizations

Padding, workspaces and per-app rules live in `komorebi.json`.

## 7. Common problems

It cannot run together with GlazeWM; WinRice asks to turn the other off. Some apps need an ignore rule.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle (runs komorebic stop).
