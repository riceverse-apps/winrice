# TranslucentTB

## 1. What it is

TranslucentTB makes the Windows taskbar transparent or blurred. It is the easiest way to get a cleaner desktop.

## 2. Who it's for

Beginners who want a quick visual change.

## 3. What changes on your screen

The taskbar becomes transparent, blurred or colored.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Enable it and right-click its tray icon to pick the style.

## 5. Main shortcuts

No shortcuts of its own.

## 6. Common customizations

Different looks for normal, maximized window, Start open and search open.

## 7. Common problems

Nothing changes: make sure the tray icon is visible. It may stop after an Explorer restart; toggle it again.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle; the taskbar goes back to normal.
