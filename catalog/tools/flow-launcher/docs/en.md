# Flow Launcher

## 1. What it is

Flow Launcher is a search box you open with a hotkey to launch apps, find files, run calculations and search the web.

## 2. Who it's for

Anyone who wants to open things quickly without the mouse.

## 3. What changes on your screen

A search box appears in the middle of the screen when you press Alt + Space.

## 4. First steps after enabling

Press Alt + Space and type an app name. Open its settings to add plugins.

## 5. Main shortcuts

See the Shortcuts tab.

## 6. Common customizations

Change the hotkey, theme and installed plugins in its settings.

## 7. Common problems

Hotkey does not work: another app (such as PowerToys Run) uses Alt + Space. Change one of them.

## 8. How to undo

Turn off the toggle.
