# Code of Conduct

WinRice is for people who want to customize Windows, including beginners. Be the kind of community you would have wanted when you were starting.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Questions that look basic are welcome.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Give reasons.
- Respect that people run different hardware, languages and levels of experience.
- Credit other people's work, and respect the licenses of the tools and themes you use or share.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sharing malware, or tools and themes designed to hide what they do, harm a computer or steal data.
- Sexual or violent content in themes, wallpapers or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, themes or contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who repeat violations. To report a problem, use the private reporting option of the repository (Security tab) or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
